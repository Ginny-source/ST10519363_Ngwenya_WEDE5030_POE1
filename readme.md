# Crave Corner

## Where Every Craving Finds Its Flavor!

## About the Business

Crave Corner is a food vendor that provides fresh, delicious, and affordable food to customers. The business offers a variety of meals, snacks, sides, and drinks while focusing on quality food and friendly service.

## Mission

Our mission is to provide fresh, delicious, and affordable food while delivering friendly and reliable service that satisfies every customer's craving.

## Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted and well-loved food business known for quality meals, excellent service, and creating memorable experiences for our customers.

## Target Audience

Our target audience includes:

- Students and young adults
- Working professionals
- Families
- Local residents
- Food lovers

## Website Purpose

The purpose of the Crave Corner website is to provide customers with an easy way to learn about the business, view the menu, place orders, make enquiries, and find contact information.

## Website Features

- Home page
- About Us page
- Menu page
- Checkout page
- Enquiry page
- Contact Us page
- Navigation menu
- Food categories
- Contact information
- Customer enquiry form
- Social media links

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
- Git
- GitHub
- Visual Studio Code

## Website Pages

### Home
Introduces Crave Corner and provides quick access to the main sections of the website.

### About Us
Provides information about the history, mission, vision, and team of Crave Corner.

### Menu
Displays the different food categories, meals, descriptions, and prices.

### Checkout
Allows customers to review their selected items and provide their details.

### Enquiries
Allows customers to submit questions and requests.

### Contact Us
Provides the business location, phone number, email address, WhatsApp details, and business hours.

## Design and Branding

The website uses a modern and welcoming food-focused design. The main branding colours are black and dark brown.

Black represents:
- Elegance
- Professionalism
- Modernity

Dark brown represents:
- Warmth
- Comfort
- Richness
- Food and natural ingredients

Cream and white can be used alongside these darker colours to create contrast and improve readability.

## Project Goals

- Increase Crave Corner's online visibility.
- Make the menu easily accessible.
- Make ordering more convenient.
- Improve communication with customers.
- Promote the business and its products.
- Provide a simple and user-friendly website.
